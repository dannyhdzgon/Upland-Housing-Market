# Upland Housing Market Analysis

## Project Overview

This project was completed for FRL 3062: Fundamentals of Real Estate at California State Polytechnic University, Pomona.

The objective was to analyze housing market trends in Upland, California, and compare local home values with broader regional and national housing markets.

Using historical housing data, I examined home price appreciation, market trends, and factors influencing housing affordability and competitiveness.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Data Analysis and Visualization

## Dataset

The analysis used historical housing market data from Zillow to compare housing values across different geographic markets.

The research focused on:

- Typical home values
- Annual home price appreciation
- Housing market trends from 2016–2026
- Comparisons with California and national housing markets
- Local housing development and market conditions

## Analysis & Methodology

1. Collected and examined historical housing market data.
2. Analyzed Upland's typical home values over a ten-year period.
3. Calculated annual home value growth rates.
4. Compared Upland housing values with California and national benchmarks.
5. Used Python and data visualizations to identify market trends.
6. Examined local development, transportation access, and housing affordability considerations.

## Key Findings

- Upland's typical home value increased approximately **73.31% between July 2016 and July 2026**.
- Home values experienced particularly strong appreciation during 2021 and 2022.
- By July 2026, Upland's typical home value was approximately **$756,988**, compared with **$738,890** for California.
- The analysis highlighted periods of rapid appreciation, market corrections, and slower growth.
